UNIT 5: TRIGONOMETRY

INTRODUCTION TO TRIGONOMETRY
Trigonometry is concerned with basic geometric objects such as triangles and circles, along with the application of periodic functions to geometric problems involving oscillations and waves.

NOTE: Trigonometric functions are crucial for engineering and can describe the movement of light and sound.

ELEMENTARY RATIOS AND EXACT VALUES
For a right-angled triangle with an angle θ, the three primary trigonometric ratios are defined by the lengths of the opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse sides.

- sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
- cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
- tan θ = opposite / adjacent

EXACT VALUES: Standard exact values for sin, cos, and tan are defined for specific angles such as 30° (π/6), 45° (π/4), and 60° (π/3).

TRIGONOMETRIC GRAPHS AND PROPERTIES
The graphs of sine, cosine, and tangent functions have distinct periodic properties and symmetries.

- Sine (y = sin x): Passes through the origin, reaches max 1 at 90°, min -1 at 270°, and has a period of 360°.
- Cosine (y = cos x): Reaches max 1 at 0°, min -1 at 180°, and has a period of 360°.
- Tangent (y = tan x): Has a period of 180° and contains vertical asymptotes where the function is undefined.

INVERSE TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS
To define inverse functions, the domains of the standard trigonometric functions must be restricted to ensure they are one-to-one.

DEFINITION: Inverse functions are denoted as sin^-1 x (arcsin), cos^-1 x (arccos), and tan^-1 x (arctan).

- The domain for sin^-1 x and cos^-1 x is -1 ≤ x ≤ 1.
- The domain for tan^-1 x is all real numbers x.

TRIGONOMETRIC IDENTITIES
Identities are equations involving trigonometric functions that are true for all values of the variables.

KEY CONCEPT: Two fundamental identities used to solve equations and prove further relations are:
1. tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
2. sin^2 θ + cos^2 θ = 1

SOLVING TRIGONOMETRIC EQUATIONS
Equations are typically solved for a specific range of values, such as 0° ≤ x ≤ 360° or 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π.

- Find the principal value using the inverse function.
- Use the unit circle or graph symmetries to find all other required solutions within the given interval.
