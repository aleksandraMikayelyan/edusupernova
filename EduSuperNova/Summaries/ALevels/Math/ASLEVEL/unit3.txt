UNIT 3: COORDINATE GEOMETRY

INTRODUCTION TO COORDINATE GEOMETRY
Coordinate geometry is the study of geometric figures by representing them using a given coordinate system. Geometric objects such as straight lines, curves, and circles can be analyzed using their algebraic representations.

COORDINATE GEOMETRY: The representation of geometric figures using coordinates in a two-dimensional plane.

LINE SEGMENTS
A line segment is the part of a line that connects two specific points.

- The length of a line segment PQ between points P(x, y) and Q(a, b) is calculated as: length PQ = √[(a - x)² + (b - y)²].
- The midpoint M of the line segment PQ is given by the formula: M = [(x + a) / 2, (y + b) / 2].

STRAIGHT LINES
The equation of a straight line relates the coordinates of any point on that line.

- The standard form of a non-vertical straight line is y = mx + c, where 'm' is the gradient and 'c' is the y-intercept.
- A vertical line has the equation x = b, where 'b' is the x-intercept.
- The point-gradient form of a line passing through point (a, b) with gradient 'm' is: y - b = m(x - a).

PARALLEL AND PERPENDICULAR LINES
The relationship between the gradients of two lines determines their geometric orientation relative to each other.

- Parallel lines have the same gradient (m₁ = m₂).
- Perpendicular lines have gradients that are negative reciprocals of each other (m₁ × m₂ = -1).

CIRCLE EQUATIONS
A circle can be represented algebraically based on its center and radius.

DEFINITION: A circle is the set of all points in a plane that are at a fixed distance (radius) from a fixed point (center).

- The equation of a circle with center (h, k) and radius 'r' is: (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r².
- To find the center and radius from a general quadratic form, use the method of completing the square.

POINTS OF INTERSECTION
Algebraic methods can be used to find where geometric figures meet.

- To find the intersection of a line and a circle, substitute the linear equation into the circle equation.
- If the resulting quadratic equation has one repeated root, the line is a tangent to the circle.
- If there are two distinct roots, the line intersects the circle at two points.

NOTE: Coordinate geometry is a foundational tool in fields like astronomy, where it is used to describe the paths of planets and other celestial objects.
