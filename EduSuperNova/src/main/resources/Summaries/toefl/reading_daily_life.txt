TOEFL SUMMARY: READING DAILY LIFE & CAMPUS SITUATIONS

1. OVERVIEW OF DAILY LIFE TEXTS:
Unlike purely academic passages, 'Daily Life' readings in the TOEFL context (often found in the Integrated tasks or Listening-to-Reading transitions) focus on university-related scenarios, such as campus policy changes, student club announcements, or housing issues.

2. STRUCTURE OF CAMPUS ANNOUNCEMENTS:
- THE PROPOSAL/CHANGE: A clear statement of what is happening (e.g., "The university will increase meal plan prices").
- THE RATIONALE: Two specific reasons for the change (e.g., 1. To improve food quality; 2. To extend cafeteria hours).

3. KEY INFORMAL & SEMI-FORMAL VOCABULARY:
- Implement: To put a plan or decision into effect.
- Logistics: The detailed coordination of a complex operation.
- Policy: A course or principle of action adopted by the university.
- Feedback: Information about reactions to a product or a person's performance of a task.

4. COMMON THEMES:
- Facilities: Renovation of libraries, gyms, or dormitories.
- Schedules: Changes to registration deadlines or exam periods.
- Community: Volunteer opportunities or local events affecting students.

5. STRATEGY FOR SUMMARY:
When summarizing daily life readings, identify the "Who, What, When, and Why." Focus on how the change impacts the community and be prepared to contrast this with a student's opinion (the speaking/writing component).
